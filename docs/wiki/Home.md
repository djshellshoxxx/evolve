# MUTAGEN Wiki

MUTAGEN is an experimental VST3 / AU / standalone instrument built around a population of synthetic cells.
Each cell has a genome, a lifespan and a voice. The population breeds, competes, infects, forms symbioses and dies,
and the player keeps the ecosystem interesting. It is an open-source audio/software-art project and is not part of
the Circuit Drift Labs commercial line (README).
